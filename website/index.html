Four words carry the whole app.

FairFriends is built around the way friends actually handle money — not ledger software.

Balance · Balance

A shared tab among a group — a trip, a flat, a birthday dinner. Everyone's part is tracked live, so “who owes what” is a glance, not a spreadsheet.

Promise · Promesa

A simple IOU between two people. One of you owes the other a fixed amount — FairFriends keeps the deadline and lets you split it across members when it's not one person's burden.

Payment · Pago

Payments land as suggestions, not surprises: the receiver accepts or rejects, and amounts can be uneven. Paid $63 of a $90 promise? That's allowed, and it's recorded.

Friendship · Amistad

Contacts join by request and accept on their side — nobody tracks money for a stranger. Everything else stays between the people who were there.

How it works.

1

Add your friends

Send a contact request. They accept — now you share a ledger, nothing more.

2

Create a balance or a promise

Group plan or one-on-one IOU — pick the shape that fits, set amounts, done.

3

Accept payments as they land

One tap confirms what came in. FairFriends updates everyone's part instantly.
